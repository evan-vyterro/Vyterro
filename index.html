Our Approach

Institutional-level execution for independent operators

Acquiring a business is one of the most complex and consequential decisions a searcher will make. Vyterro works alongside operators throughout the full acquisition lifecycle — bringing the discipline of institutional deal teams to independent searchers.

Vyterro is intentionally selective, working with a small number of searchers each year who demonstrate discipline, capability, and long-term ambition.

  1. Deal sourcing & opportunity evaluation

  2. Underwriting & deal analysis

  3. Negotiation strategy

  4. Transaction structuring

  5. Due diligence coordination

  6. Lender & capital coordination

  7. Closing preparation & execution

  8. Transition & post-close support

Our Offerings

Three distinct ways to work with Vyterro

Vyterro Deal Room, Vyterro Partnerships, and Advisory Services are separate engagements built for different needs, each with its own structure, access, and cost.

01

Core Offer

Vyterro Deal Room

A small, curated group of searchers who are actively working toward a deal and want to get better at executing. This isn't a passive group.

For active searchers who know the basics and want to get sharper at evaluating, structuring, and executing — not passive investors or people just kicking the tires.

What's Included

  • A weekly call on real, live deals — structuring, negotiating, diligence, and the decisions that matter.

  • Direct access to the founder through Slack, voice notes, and Loom.

  • A small, high-caliber group learning from each other's live deals.

  • The templates, frameworks, and checklists used day to day.

  • Access to the network — lenders, attorneys, diligence providers, and recruiters.

Cost

  • Core Membership

    $2,000/mo, month to month. Price rises as the room fills.

  • 1:1 Calls (add-on)

    Optional dedicated time.

  • Deal Sourcing (add-on)

    Optional, if available.

Vyterro Partnerships

Vyterro works alongside the searcher directly on the deal, treating it as partially its own — sourcing, structuring, negotiation, diligence, financing, and post-close.

  • Equity, no monthly fee — Vyterro takes an ownership stake instead of charging a fee.

  • Selective, interview required — fit matters given the long-term, equity nature of the arrangement.
