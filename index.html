Our Offerings

Three distinct ways to work with Vyterro

Deal Room, Partnership, and Advisory are separate engagements built for different needs — each with its own structure, access, and cost.

Core Offer

Deal Room

A small, curated group of searchers actively looking for a business who want to get better at executing. Members don't need to have bought a business before, but should already understand how acquisitions work and be actively working toward one. It is not a passive group.

What's Included

  • Weekly call — real deals covering structuring, negotiating, diligence, key decision milestones, and market dynamics.

  • Direct access to the founder via Slack, voice notes, and Loom videos.

  • Small curated group — learning from other members in real, live deals; high-caliber network.

  • Templates, frameworks & checklists — the actual tools used day-to-day, built to flex for creative deal structures.

  • Network access — lenders, attorneys, diligence providers, recruiters, and post-closing third parties.

Who it's for

  • ✓Actively searching for a business
  • ✓Understands the basics of acquisitions and how deals work
  • ✓Trying to get better at evaluating, structuring, and executing
  • ✓Sees this as a long-term path — multiple deals, roll-ups, investing

Who it's not for

  • ×People just exploring the idea
  • ×Passive investors
  • ×Anyone not actively working toward a deal

Equity Model

Partnership

A true partnership model: Vyterro works alongside the searcher directly on the deal, treating it as partially its own — sourcing strategy, search narrative refinement, deal evaluation, broker/seller calls, structuring, negotiation, diligence, bank and closing process support, transition support, and post-closing advisory.

  • Equity, no monthly fee — Vyterro takes an ownership stake in the acquisition instead of charging a fee.

  • No capital invested — Vyterro does not invest capital and is not diluted by capital raises, but can help source capital.

  • Selective, interview required — fit matters given the long-term, equity nature of the arrangement.

Meet the Owner

The founder behind Vyterro

Evan DiLeonardi

Founder & Managing Partner

Evan DiLeonardi builds his career around acquiring and operating cash-flowing businesses across services and manufacturing, often partnering directly with operators using his own capital. Separately, he founded and manages Meridian Peak Capital, an acquisition fund that backs proven operators buying small and lower-middle-market businesses.

He's not interested in short-term momentum or transactional deal-making — Vyterro reflects that same approach: a small, high-signal group built for searchers who think in years, not quarters.
